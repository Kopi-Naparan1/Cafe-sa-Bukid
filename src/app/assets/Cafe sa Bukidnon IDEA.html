About the Cafe

Built for slow coffee, good meals, and mountain air.

Cafe sa Bukid brings together a relaxed countryside setting and a menu shaped around everyday favorites. The goal is simple: make the place feel easy to stay in, whether you came for breakfast, coffee, or an afternoon reset.
